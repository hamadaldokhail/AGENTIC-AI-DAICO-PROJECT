# About the DAICO Center

## Mission
The Data & AI Center of Excellence (DAICO) at King Saud University exists to build national capability in data and artificial intelligence. In partnership with SDAIA, DAICO trains students and professionals to design, build, and deploy responsible AI systems that serve Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 goals.

## What DAICO offers
- Instructor-led, hands-on courses spanning the full AI stack: foundations, data engineering, machine learning, deep learning, NLP, computer vision, MLOps, and agentic AI.
- Project-based capstones that require learners to ship working systems, not just slides.
- An open, community-oriented culture: learners are encouraged to publish their work on GitHub and contribute to the Saudi open-source community.

## The Agentic AI cohort
Building Agentic AI Systems (AGT-401) is DAICO's most advanced language-track course. Over five days learners study agents and tool calling, sub-agents and routing, retrieval-augmented generation, LangGraph workflows, short- and long-term memory, human-in-the-loop control, reliability, and observability — then prove mastery in a graded capstone.

## Sponsorship
DAICO's training is sponsored by SDAIA, the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority, which sets the national data and AI agenda and underwrites learner seats so that training is free at the point of access.
