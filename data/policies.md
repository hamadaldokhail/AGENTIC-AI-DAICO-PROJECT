# DAICO Center - Enrollment & Certification Policies

## Eligibility and Enrollment
- DAICO courses are open to KSU students and to the public under SDAIA sponsorship.
- A learner may only enroll in a course if they have completed all listed prerequisites.
- Enrollment is completed through the DAICO learner portal. After a seat is reserved, the learner receives a confirmation email within 24 hours.
- Each course has a fixed number of seats. When `seats_available` reaches zero, the course is full and new learners are placed on a waitlist.

## Attendance
- A minimum of **80% attendance** is required to be eligible for the completion certificate.
- Sessions run for 5 days. Missing more than one full day makes a learner ineligible for the certificate for that cohort.

## Certification
- Learners receive an official **SDAIA Academy certificate** after they (1) meet the attendance requirement and (2) pass the course assessment.
- For courses that include a capstone (such as AGT-401, Building Agentic AI Systems), the certificate additionally requires a **passing capstone grade of 60 out of 100**, with **no single rubric section scoring below 40% of its points**.

## Withdrawal and Refunds
- Because seats are sponsored by SDAIA, there is no tuition fee. A learner may withdraw up to **3 days before the course start date** to release their seat to a waitlisted learner.
- Withdrawing after the course has started forfeits certificate eligibility for that cohort.

## Location and Schedule
- The DAICO center is located at **King Saud University, Riyadh**.
- Courses run Sunday to Thursday. Times vary by course and are listed in the course catalog.

## GitHub and Documentation Requirement
- Every capstone project must be published to the learner's own active GitHub account with a professional README, meaningful incremental commits, and a `.gitignore` that excludes secrets and generated files.
- The repository must state the training programme name and cohort dates, and link to SDAIA Academy's GitHub where relevant.
