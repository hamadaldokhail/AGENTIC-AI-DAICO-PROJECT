# DAICO Center — Frequently Asked Questions

## What is DAICO?
DAICO is the Data & AI Center of Excellence at King Saud University (KSU). It delivers hands-on AI and data training and is sponsored by SDAIA, the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority.

## Do the courses cost anything?
No. Course seats are sponsored by SDAIA, so there is no tuition fee for accepted learners.

## Who can enroll?
Both KSU students and members of the public may enroll, subject to meeting each course's prerequisites.

## What certificate do I get?
Learners who meet the 80% attendance requirement and pass the assessment receive an official SDAIA Academy certificate of completion.

## How do prerequisites work?
Each course lists the courses that must be completed first. For example, Applied Machine Learning (ML-201) requires Foundations of Artificial Intelligence (AI-101). You cannot enroll until the prerequisites are met.

## What is the capstone project?
Advanced courses such as Building Agentic AI Systems (AGT-401) end with a capstone. The capstone is graded out of 100 and requires a minimum of 60 to pass, with no section below 40% of its points.

## Which programming language is used?
Most courses use Python. Foundations of Artificial Intelligence (AI-101) requires no prior programming experience.

## Where is the center located?
The DAICO center is on the King Saud University campus in Riyadh.

## How do I get help during a course?
Each cohort has an assigned instructor and teaching assistants. Learners can also ask the DAICO Navigator assistant about courses, prerequisites, seat availability, policies, and capstone guidance.

## Is there a waitlist if a course is full?
Yes. When a course shows zero available seats, you are added to a waitlist and notified if a seat is released.
